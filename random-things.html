Random Things

Me and my best friend — the person who has always been there for me through every laugh, every tear, and every late-night adventure. This photo was taken one evening when we were heading home late, but we couldn’t resist capturing the moment. It’s not just a picture, it’s a memory of how we always find joy even in the simplest times, proving that friendship makes even the longest nights brighter.

Monster — my favorite drink! It gave me energy during long days, especially in ROTC training, but sometimes it was also the reason I nearly blacked out from exhaustion. Still, it became part of my routine, a funny reminder of how I pushed myself beyond limits while laughing at my own struggles. It’s more than just a drink; it’s a symbol of my determination and resilience, even when I was tired and worn out.

C.A.T. moments — unforgettable times when we were the uppers guiding our lowers. I miss the chaos: the shouting, the rushing, the endless tasks. I was known as the “dragon” because I was always strict, but deep down those moments were filled with camaraderie, discipline, and laughter that bonded us forever. It taught me leadership, patience, and the importance of teamwork, even when things felt overwhelming.

Batangas Lomi — my ultimate comfort food. The rich noodles, the savory broth, and the squeeze of calamansi with soy make it perfect. I always eat this before heading back to Baguio, and it reminds me of home, family, and the flavors that bring me happiness every single time. It’s not just food, it’s a tradition that warms my heart and connects me to my roots.

Thirst trap vibes — that’s me, with a filter of course. Even if it’s just a simple selfie, it’s a reminder that sometimes we need to appreciate ourselves, laugh at our imperfections, and celebrate the little ways we can feel beautiful. It’s not about perfection, it’s about confidence, fun, and embracing who we are in every moment.
